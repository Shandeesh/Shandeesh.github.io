Reading the log trail.

Simulated SOC exercises, worked end to end: detection, correlation, classification, and containment recommendations.

CRITICAL
auth.log · vpn.log · firewall.log · powershell.log
Unauthorized login & VPN compromise

Correlated repeated authentication failures followed by a successful login with unusual VPN access and external IP activity. Follow-on PowerShell commands showed system discovery, account enumeration, and AD reconnaissance, plus signs of file archiving consistent with data exfiltration. Recommended account disablement, session termination, source-IP blocking, password reset, and stronger MFA/EDR controls.
